DevFlow Automation Engine

An intelligent media generation and automated publishing platform designed to create, synthesize, render, and publish educational videos and short-form media to YouTube and social platforms.

About DevFlow Automation Engine

DevFlow Automation Engine is an automated workflow utility created and maintained by Naman Jain. The application automates the full-stack pipeline of video production:

  • AI Script Synthesis: Generates concise educational and technical scripts adhering to strict pedagogical structures.
  • Voiceover Synthesis: Uses cutting-edge multimodal text-to-speech to render high-clarity spoken audio.
  • Media Assembly: Dynamically compiles royalty-free visual assets, animated slides, and synchronized audio into 9:16 vertical video assets using FFmpeg.
  • Automated Distribution: Integrates directly with YouTube Data API v3 to upload scheduled Shorts, publish descriptions, and manage pin comments on user-authorized channels.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how DevFlow Automation Engine collects, uses, protects, and handles your information when you connect and use our application in conjunction with Google APIs.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you authenticate with DevFlow Automation Engine via Google OAuth 2.0, the application requests access to specific scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: Allows the engine to upload your generated video content directly to your authorized YouTube channel as a Public or Unlisted Short.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl: Allows the engine to retrieve video metadata and post video comments containing supplementary educational links or references.

We only store OAuth2 authentication tokens (Refresh Tokens and temporary Access Tokens) necessary to perform automated uploads on your behalf.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Your Google user data is strictly utilized for the following functional purposes:

  • Authenticating your channel identity via YouTube Data API v3.
  • Uploading rendered video files directly to your connected YouTube channel.
  • Setting video titles, descriptions, tags, and category IDs.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
DevFlow Automation Engine's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do NOT sell, lease, trade, or transfer your personal data or Google OAuth tokens to any third parties. All data is used strictly within the closed automation pipeline. No Google user data is provided to data brokers, advertising networks, or external analytics firms.

4. Data Storage and Security

All sensitive credentials and OAuth tokens are stored in secure, encrypted environment variables and access-controlled databases. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for all network communications with Google APIs.

5. User Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over your account. You can revoke DevFlow Automation Engine's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Security Settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the application can no longer access your YouTube channel or upload videos.
